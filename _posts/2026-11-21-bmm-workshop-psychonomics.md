At this year's Psychonomic Society meeting in San Diego, I co-led a hands-on workshop on the BMM package together with Gidon and Chenyu. We put the session together as a practical introduction for researchers who want to move from conceptual Bayesian modeling into actually fitting and interpreting models in their own work.

The workshop walked participants through the core workflow of the package: setting up a model, estimating parameters, and reading the output in a way that connects back to substantive psychological questions. We designed the materials so people could follow along live, ask questions as we went, and leave with something they could reuse after the conference.

Preparing the workshop with Gidon and Chenyu was a real treat. We spent time refining examples, anticipating where newcomers usually get stuck, and making sure the session felt collaborative rather than lecture-heavy. Sharing the package with the Psychonomics community in that format was a highlight of the meeting for me.
