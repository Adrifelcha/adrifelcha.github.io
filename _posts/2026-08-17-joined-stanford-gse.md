In August 2026, I joined the Stanford Graduate School of Education as a postdoctoral scholar. I am part of the [SPARK Lab](https://sparklab.stanford.edu/){:target="_blank"} (Stanford Project on Adaptation and Resilience in Kids) at the Stanford Center for Early Childhood, working with Dr. Jelena Obradović.

I was hired as part of the [MAGIC project](https://sparklab.stanford.edu/magic){:target="_blank"} ([Measuring Acquisition & Growth of Inquiry & Curiosity](https://obradovic-spark.github.io/){:target="_blank"}), which develops performance-based assessment tasks of young learners' curiosity, creative thinking, problem-solving, and scientific inquiry. My work on the project focuses on building robust, scalable measurement tools that can support research and practice in early childhood education.
